Frequently asked questions

What is Family Command Center?

It's an all-in-one household management app for families — shared tasks and chores, a family calendar, budgeting and bill tracking with optional bank sync, kids' allowances and rewards, school and health tracking, smart home control, and an AI assistant that can answer questions using your family's real data.

How do I add family members?

From the Family tab, tap "Add Member" to create a profile for a spouse, co-parent, or child. Each adult can optionally get their own login; children are managed as profiles under the parent/guardian account unless you set up a linked child device through Guardian mode.

Is my bank information safe?

Bank connections are handled through Plaid, a service used by thousands of financial apps. Family Command Center never sees or stores your bank login credentials — Plaid securely relays your account balances and transactions to us. You can disconnect a linked account at any time from Finance settings.

What's included in the free plan vs. Premium / Family Pro?

The free plan covers the core household tools for a small family. Premium adds unlimited AI queries and bank syncing across more accounts; Family Pro adds Military Mode, the Family Digital Twin AI feature, priority support, and support for larger families. You can compare plans and upgrade any time from Settings → Subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, so cancellation happens there too: on iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions; on Android, open the Google Play Store → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals — you keep access until the end of the current billing period.

What is Guardian mode and how does location sharing work?

Guardian mode lets a parent pair a child's device to see its location, set geofence zones (like home or school), and manage basic screen-time controls. It's opt-in: nothing is tracked until a parent explicitly pairs a device, and it can be turned off at any time from the Guardian Dashboard.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Settings → Account and choose "Delete Account," or contact us at the email above and we'll take care of it. This permanently removes your family's data from our servers.
